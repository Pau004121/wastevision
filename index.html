How to take good training photos

  1. Use one whole item (not a pile of different materials).
  2. Capture front, back, left, right, top, and 45° views.
  3. Keep the item fully visible in every shot.
  4. Take some images with different lighting and backgrounds.
  5. Use only clean, dry, non-hazardous items. Never handle sharp waste.
  6. Do not photograph people, identification cards, or private spaces.
Research workflow: Each set receives a pending status. Only the project owner can review submissions in the Supabase Dashboard. Uploaded photos do not retrain the model automatically.
